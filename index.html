How we met

## Our Story Sometimes, the most beautiful stories begin when we least expect them to. Our journey started with a biodata shared through our families. At the time, I wasn’t really ready to think about marriage, so I didn’t pay much attention. But among all the biodatas was a picture of a charming, innocent-looking girl who somehow caught my attention — **Anushka**. A simple LinkedIn message, a conversation between our families, and eventually our first meeting brought us together. I wasn’t sure what to expect that day, but as we started talking, things slowly began to change. From WhatsApp conversations to long daily calls, we started discovering each other — our dreams, families, values, little habits, and everything in between. With time, we found comfort in each other, understood each other better, and became a part of each other’s everyday lives. Anushka’s warmth, patience, and understanding made our bond even stronger. We laughed together, shared countless little moments, and gradually found ourselves falling in love — not just with each other, but with each other’s families too. What began as a simple family introduction became a beautiful journey of friendship, understanding, trust, and love. And today, we’re excited to begin the next chapter together. **Our story started with a meeting. Our forever starts here. ❤️**

Events

1st December

Rang-e-Mehandi12PM - 4PM

It's one of the oldest traditional Indian pre wedding ceremony where the bride and groom attend the event together and a professional mehndi artist applies mehndi to the bride's hands and feet. This will be having a celebratory festival feel to it with people wearing vibrant clothes and dancing, singing, playing games and a lot more.

Cocktail Night 7PM - 12AM

This event is no way close to ethnic. An evening filled with music, laughter, and unforgettable performances. From heartfelt family acts to energetic dance performances, the Sangeet is all about celebrating the joy of two families coming together. With booze flowing down the floor and the western attire on, everyone would be busy showing off their new moves on the stage which they have finely honed.

2nd December

The Turmeric tale10AM-12PM

A beautiful morning filled with love, laughter, and the vibrant colours of tradition. Surrounded by family and loved ones, we’ll celebrate this special ritual as we prepare for the big day ahead.

Shahi Sawari6PM-12AM

This is the main event in the entire wedding when groom comes with the barat and is greeted by the bride side with some dhol of course as maximum number of guests would be attending this event.

Saat Vachan2AM-4AM

The most sacred and meaningful moment of our celebration. With our loved ones by our side, we’ll take the seven sacred vows around the holy fire, promising to walk together through every chapter of life — with love, trust, and companionship.
